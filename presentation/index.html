Project 2 • DMN Decision Brain: Loan Eligibility Rules

Business Rules Table (loan_eligibility_rules.dmn) • Hit Policy: First

Camunda DMN Table: Credit Scoring & Risk Tier
Hit Policy: First 3 Input Columns 3 Output Columns Deterministic Risk Routing
How the Credit Engine Decides
Camunda evaluates Credit Score (CIBIL), Debt-to-Income (DTI), and Loan Quantum in real-time to assign risk grade and determine whether human intervention is needed.
Rule Breakdown (When → Then)
Rule 1: Tier A Prime
Credit Score ≥ 750 + DTI ≤ 40% + Loan ≤ $50k → Auto-Approve (Rate: 8.5%).
Rule 2: Tier B Near-Prime
Credit Score 680–749 + DTI ≤ 45% → Underwriter Review (Rate: 10.2%).
Rule 3: High DTI Counter
Credit Score ≥ 700 + DTI > 45% → Counter-Offer (Reduced principal or guarantor required).
Rule 4: High Value
Loan Amount > $100k → Senior Credit Committee dual authorization.
Rule 5: Sub-Prime Decline
Credit Score < 650 or DTI > 60% → Immediate Decline.
🎤 Presenter Script (Say this out loud)
"Notice how Rule 3 handles high DTI: Instead of outright declining a customer whose debt ratio exceeds 45%, the DMN triggers a Counter-Offer. The bank can offer a lower loan amount or request a guarantor, preserving the customer relationship while protecting the bank's credit risk."

Master Architecture Synthesis & Viva Defense Checklist

Ready-Made Answers to Common Evaluator & Professor Questions

Q: Why Use DMN Instead of Code?

Answer: Hardcoding if-else statements in Java requires recompiling and redeploying the entire workflow for every policy change. DMN externalizes decision rules into a visual table that risk managers or academic chairs can edit directly at runtime without redeployment.

Demonstrated In
Project 1: Guide Quotas • Project 2: Credit Scoring
Q: How Are Loops Prevented?

Answer: Both models enforce finite iteration guards using retry counter gateways. Project 1 restricts revision cycles to ≤ 2 and guide invitations to ≤ 3. Project 2 restricts counter-offers to ≤ 1. If limits are exceeded, the process routes to an executive escalation or rejection.

Demonstrated In
Gateways F1, F5, F9 in P1 • Gateway L5/L6 in P2
Q: What is Compensation in BPMN?

Answer: Unlike an error catch that aborts a task, BPMN Compensation performs business state rollback for already-completed activities. When a student withdraws or a loan is cancelled, compensation automatically frees reserved guide quotas and refunds origination fees.
